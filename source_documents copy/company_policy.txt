ACME Corp Work-From-Home and Remote Work Policy
Last Updated: January 2026

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to apply for remote work privileges. Approval is subject to manager sign-off and performance review metrics.

2. Equipment Stipend
The company provides a one-time remote work setup stipend of $500. This stipend can be used to purchase ergonomic office chairs, external monitors, or keyboards. Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of purchase.

3. Core Working Hours
All remote team members must be online and reachable via Slack and email during our core operating hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST). 

4. Internet Requirements
Employees must maintain a reliable high-speed internet connection with a minimum download speed of 50 Mbps. ACME Corp will reimburse up to $50 per month for home internet expenses upon presentation of an official monthly bill.
